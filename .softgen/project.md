# School Management Website

## Vision
A full-stack, mobile-responsive school management and information website for a primary school (Baby Class to 5th Standard) serving underprivileged families. Provides public information about the school's mission, academics, and activities, while enabling admin management of notices, inquiries, and gallery.

**Target users:**
- Parents (mobile-first, limited data)
- Public visitors exploring the school
- School administrator (Sister)

## Design
Color palette:
- `--primary: 217 91% 60%` (bright blue)
- `--accent: 45 93% 58%` (sunny yellow) 
- `--success: 142 71% 45%` (fresh green)
- `--background: 210 40% 98%` (light blue-tinted white)
- `--foreground: 222 47% 11%` (deep blue-gray)
- `--muted: 210 40% 96%` (soft blue-gray)

Fonts: Fredoka (headings, rounded, playful), Nunito (body, clean, warm)

Style: Warm and nurturing primary school aesthetic — bright blue and yellow create an optimistic learning environment. Sun + Book logo symbolizes knowledge and growth. Rounded cards, generous spacing for mobile readability, CSS-only animations, data-efficient design.

## Features
**Public:**
- Homepage with hero, mission, latest notices ticker
- About Us (history, community mission, Principal message)
- Academics (Early Years, Primary levels with tabs/cards)
- Gallery (grid of school activities, events, classroom photos)
- Admissions (application process, fee structure, inquiry form)
- Contact Us (form, phone, email, Google Maps)

**Admin Dashboard:**
- Post/edit/delete notices (notice board management)
- View/manage admission inquiries (lead management)
- View contact form submissions
- Upload gallery images with categories
- Protected by email/password authentication
